## The doctrine
The Network's model, in its own course materials: physician-regulated dispatch; advanced and
physician-staffed ambulances; motorcycle first responders; prehospital RSI, tranexamic acid and
**whole blood**; stabilization units with damage-control theatres; written stabilize-and-transfer
protocols with the receiving hospitals; a trauma registry; the Escuela Hora Dorada.
