## The plan (Bastías's pitch, March 2005)
1. **Flagship** in the civic district: ED with resus bays, two ORs, a small ICU, imaging, the
   ambulance base and HQ. Estimated **120,000–170,000 UF** all-in.
2. **Stabilization units** across Santiago (2–4 resus bays, a damage-control OR, a few ICU beds,
   2 ambulances each), from 2007.
3. **Ambulances** linking them: advanced crews, a doctor on the critical ones.
4. Revenue: Isapre and mutual patients, Ley de Urgencia billing, transfers, rescue
   subscriptions, contracts (events, companies, the state in a crisis).

## Shareholders' pact (13 Apr 2005), key terms
- Simple majority for ordinary decisions; **75% for a new shareholder, sale of the company, or sale of the flagship building**.
- Sweat equity: 2% each to Contreras and Rivas from Bastías's stake, vesting over 3 years.
- MedSur preferred supplier for 3 years at or below market; new equipment with manufacturer's warranty only.
- Tag-along, drag-along, right of first refusal, 2-year non-compete, buy-out at book value on death or disability (insured).
