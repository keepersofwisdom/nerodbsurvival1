## The plan (Pietro's model, January 2025)
- **What:** a stabilization centre on the Hora Dorada model, built for 2025: 2 resus bays, 6
  boxes, 1 damage-control theatre, 4 critical beds, 2 advanced ambulances and 2 motorcycles, a
  telemedicine and triage app (Pietro's), point-of-care ultrasound in every box, whole blood on
  site.
- **Where:** central Santiago, within ten minutes of the big trauma receivers (Posta Central, the
  Hospital del Trabajador, Clínica Santa María, the Salvador).
- **Money:**
  - All-in ≈ **70,000–90,000 UF** for a conversion, plus ≈ 20,000 UF of working capital.
  - Break-even ≈ **11,000 UF a month** collected.
  - Sources: the partners' 8,500 UF, an investor (≈ 35,000 UF for the reserved 30%), a bank loan
    against property, equipment leasing.
- **Revenue:**
  - work accidents at night (talks with a mutual, which is Marcela's employer: a conflict);
  - Isapre and SOAP patients;
  - Ley de Urgencia stabilizations;
  - UGCC critical beds;
  - event medicine;
  - transfers.
- **Target opening:** March 2026.
