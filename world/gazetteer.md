# GAZETTEER — places in play

Real places are described in depth in `research/06_gazetteer_central_santiago.md` (districts in
2005 / 2019 / 2025, the hospital directory, travel times, property prices) and
`research/03_underground_santiago.md` (what lies beneath). This file is the quick sheet for play,
plus the **fictional places** the campaign adds (filled in as they're created).

---

## 1. The heart (within 2 km of Plaza Italia)

| Place | What it is | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Plaza Italia / Plaza Baquedano** | The roundabout where the Alameda becomes Providencia; Metro Baquedano (L1 1977, L5 1997) | The city's party and protest square; epicentre of 2019; the statue removed in 2021; a "Nueva Plaza Italia" redesign from 2024 |
| **Hospital del Trabajador** (ACHS), Ramón Carnicer 185 | The work-accident mutual's trauma hospital (1971); burns, spine, hand; a new basement ED on floor −1 in 2025 | Around the corner from Plaza Italia; the fiction's bunkers lie "around" it |
| **Posta Central** (HUAP), Portugal 125 | Chile's first emergency hospital (1911); the public trauma and burns reference | The busiest, most legendary ED in the country; 1.5–2 km from Plaza Italia |
| **Hospital Clínico UC**, Marcoleta 367 | The Catholic university's elite academic hospital | Next to the Posta |
| **Remodelación San Borja** | 1969 tower estate with **4–7.5 km of walkable service tunnels**, wells and a boiler plant (COSSBO) | A real hidden lifeline beside the Posta |
| **Barrio Lastarria / GAM / Parque Forestal** | Bohemian quarter; the GAM is the ex-Edificio Diego Portales (Junta HQ 1973–81) | Front line in 2019; a natural place for a unit or the founder's flat |
| **Cerro Santa Lucía** | The founding hill; solid rock in the old centre | At its foot, Santa Lucía 162: the ex-DINA clinic, now a national monument |
| **Barrio Bustamante** | Mid-rise flats, the long park, students and nurses | Staff housing; a walk to everything |
| **Clínica Santa María / Clínica Indisa** | Big private clinics across the river (Av. Santa María) | Isapre patients' destinations; Frei Montalva died at Santa María (1982) |
| **Hospital del Salvador / Tórax / Asenjo / Calvo Mackenna** | The public hospital cluster down Providencia / Salvador | The ocular trauma unit (2019); old pavilion tunnels vacated when the new hospital opens (2026) |
| **Hospital Metropolitano** (ex-Hospital Militar), Holanda 060 | Where Pinochet died (2006); MINSAL's from 2009, lease to ~2026 | Sub-basements against Costanera Center's deepest levels |

## 2. The civic district

| Place | What it is | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **La Moneda** and the **Plaza de la Constitución** | The palace; under the plaza, the 1930s vault (SICAR's "El Hoyo", 1974) beside the Agustinas 1220 car park, and the 1982–83 "búnker" hall (Auditorio Prieto), restored 2025–26 | The real anchor of every tunnel rumour |
| **Plaza de la Ciudadanía / Centro Cultural La Moneda** | Three underground levels (opened Dec 2005 / Jan 2006) | Legitimate underground beside the palace |
| **Morandé 80** | The side door through which Allende's body left; reopened 2003 | Memory |
| **Ministerio de Hacienda, Banco Central, ex-Hotel Carrera** | Ministries and vaults around the plaza | Rumoured tunnels (legend) |
| **Barrio Yungay / Libertad station** | Old working-class barrio west of the centre; Line 5's finished, never-opened station | A sealed platform under the barrio |
