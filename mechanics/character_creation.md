# CHARACTER CREATION — THE FOUNDER AND THE HEIR

The campaign has two player characters, one after the other:

| Era | Character | When | Age |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I — Los Cimientos** (The Foundations) | **The Founder**: emergency physician, prepper, builder of the Network | 2005 → ~2025 | 35 → ~55 |
| **II — La Herencia** (The Inheritance) | **The Heir**: a young emergency physician who discovers the Network's depths and keeps it going | ~2025 → | ~28 → |
| **III — El Fin** (The End) | The Heir (and whoever is left) | when it comes | — |

The DM runs creation **conversationally**, one or two steps per message, with flavour and
options. Nothing the player wants to choose is rolled. When done, the DM writes
`characters/player.md`, sets `world/state.json`, creates the NPCs in
`characters/npc_roster.md` (secrets in `npc_secrets.md`), and only then writes
`dm/arc_1_bible.md`.

# PART A — THE FOUNDER (Era I)

## Step 1 — Who he is
- **Name** (Chilean names carry class: both surnames, a nickname).
- **Born** ~1970 (35 in 2005). Where: which comuna, which city.
- **Family and class:** old money, professional middle class, a *población*, immigrant (German,
  Palestinian, Croatian, Italian, Basque), military family? What were his parents in 1973?
  (He was three at the coup, eight in 1978, fifteen at the **3 March 1985** earthquake,
  eighteen at the 1988 plebiscite.)
- **School:** a state *liceo* (Instituto Nacional, Lastarria, the Liceo de Aplicación), a
  Catholic school, an English or German school, a regional school.
- **Medical school** (~1988–1995): Universidad de Chile, Pontificia Universidad Católica, U. de
  Concepción, U. de Valparaíso, U. de La Frontera, U. Austral, abroad.

## Step 2 — How he became an emergency physician
Emergency medicine was brand new in Chile in the 1990s (see
`research/05_emergency_medicine_ems_disaster.md`). Choose a path:

| Path | What it gives | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **One of the first Chilean residents** | Pioneer, knows everyone in the specialty, fought the surgeons for respect | Underpaid years; old enemies among the "real" specialists |
| **Trained abroad** (USA, Spain, France, Israel, Argentina, Canada) | Ahead of his time; languages; a foreign network | An outsider; *se cree gringo*; credential battles with CONACEM |
| **Self-made at the Posta Central** | Ten years of the worst nights in Chile; street credibility | No formal title at first, or a late one; rough edges |
| **Military or SAMU background** | Discipline, prehospital and disaster skill, uniform contacts | The dictatorship's shadow, or the state's bureaucracy, on him |

## Step 3 — The ten years before (1995–2005)
Pick **two** (or invent): the Posta Central's nights · Hospital del Trabajador's trauma ·
SAMU Metropolitano's ambulances · a mining camp in the Atacama · Antarctica · an NGO or a war
abroad (the Balkans, Africa, Haiti) · a disaster abroad (a tsunami, an earthquake) · a
fellowship in disaster medicine · a private clinic's ED · teaching at a university · a term in
the Colegio Médico's politics. Each gives skill points (Step 6) and **one NPC tie**.

## Step 4 — Why he prepares
The core of the man. What made him a prepper?
- A **formative disaster** (the 1985 earthquake at fifteen; a fire; a flood; a war seen abroad)
- A **fear** with a name (nuclear war, collapse, a pandemic, the sun, the fault under Santiago,
  the state turning on its people again)
- A **person** who taught him (a grandfather who lived through 1939 Chillán, an uncle who hid
  people in 1973, a mentor abroad)
- What does he *believe* is coming, and when?

## Step 5 — Attributes
Distribute **62 points** among **Body, Hands, Nerve, Presence, Wits** (min 6, max 17; an 18 needs
a reason in his story).

## Step 6 — Traits, drives, skills
**Traits:** all pairs start at 10/10. Move up to **8 points** (each point raises one side, lowers
the other). The DM suggests nudges from his story (a prepper: Paranoid, Cautious; a Posta
veteran: Detached, Calm).

**Drives:** **Preparation 15** (he wouldn't be the founder without it) and **Duty (patients)
12**, plus **two or three of his own** at 15, 12 and 10 (Love for someone, Fear of something,
Guilt for a death, Ambition, Faith, Vengeance…).

**Skills:** start from the **EM specialist package**, then add the Step 3 packages, then **25

## Step 7 — Life in 2005
- **Home:** where he lives (comuna, house or flat), what he drives.
- **Love:** single, married, separated, a lover? Children (ages)?
- **Parents and siblings:** alive? close?
- **One friend, one rival, one debt** (to someone, or someone owes him).
- **One secret** (anything: an affair, a death he caused, a drug habit, a past in politics, a
  child nobody knows about).

## Step 8 — The Network's beginning
- **The idea** in his own words (the pitch he gives investors).
- **The money:** family, banks, partners, an Isapre or clinic group, foreign capital, the Old Men
  (see `enterprise.md §7`). How much, and on what terms.
- **The flagship site** (the DM offers real locations from the gazetteer) and the first one or
  two stabilization units planned.
- **The name** of the company and the clinic.
- **The first people:** a partner, a head nurse, an ambulance chief, a lawyer, an engineer.
- **The first bunker:** does he plan to build, or does he already know of something beneath the
  city? (The DM may reveal less than he expects.)

# PART B — THE HEIR (Era II)

Created **before Era I ends**, or at least sketched now, so that the DM can **seed the heir
into the founder's years** (a child patient on 27F, the nurse's daughter, a student at a
course, a face in a crowd at Baquedano in 2019) without the player necessarily knowing which
moment it was.

Sketch now:
- **Name, gender, born** ~1997 (13 at 27F, 22 at the estallido, 23 when COVID hits, 28 in 2025).
- **Family and class**, school, medical school (~2015–2022), EM residency (~2022–2025).
- **Personality** in three words. **Why emergency medicine.**
- **Their tie to the founder** (or none, and they discover it): family, student, patient,
  colleague, stranger, enemy's child.
- **What they know** about the Network and the bunkers at the start of Era II (probably
  nothing).

Full creation (attributes, traits, skills) happens at the start of Era II, using the same
steps, with a **resident's package** (EM specialist package −2 in each clinical skill) and
experiences from their own life (27F as a child, the estallido as a student, COVID as an intern).
