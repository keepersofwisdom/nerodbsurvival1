# THE TURN — QUARTERS, YEARS AND THE YEARS BETWEEN

Twenty years pass before the heir arrives, and more before the end. Time is handled at three
speeds:

| Speed | When | What runs |
|---|---|---|
| **Scene by scene** | A chapter in play: a shift, a negotiation, a disaster | Normal play |
| **The Quarter** | At the end of each quarter in play (31 Mar, 30 Jun, 30 Sep, 31 Dec) | Steps 1–6 below, brief |
| **The Year** | On 31 December (with that quarter) | All steps, plus Step 7 |
| **The Years Between** | A fast-forward over a year or more (between chapters) | One compressed Year per year skipped, plus a **vignette** each year |

Delegate the bookkeeping and the rolls to the **`world-keeper`** agent. Present the result to
the player as a short chronicle ("**The Year Turns — 2007**"), with rolls in a compact table and
any letters, messages or news items written in full.

---

## Step 1 — The world moves
- Check `world/timeline.md` for the period: mark historical events ✔ (or ✘ with a divergence
  number) and describe how they touched the founder's life and the Network.
- Advance **off-screen NPCs**: careers, marriages, illnesses, deaths, betrayals, promotions.
  Update `characters/npc_roster.md`.
- **News and rumours** reaching the founder: 1–3 per quarter, some false, some about him.

## Step 2 — The Network
Run `enterprise.md §5`: the books, the Management roll, unit incidents, key staff. Advance
projects and threat clocks. Update `world/network.md`.

## Step 3 — The bunkers
Run `preparedness.md §3–5`: project clocks, stores rotation, condition, **Exposure**. Update
`world/bunkers.md` (and the DM-side truth in `dm/`).

## Step 4 — Body and mind
- **Stress:** recover what the quarter allowed (holidays, family time, therapy); add what it
  cost. Check thresholds (`rules.md §9.1`).
- **Health:** ongoing wounds and illnesses; habits catching up (drink, smoking, no sleep);
  occupational risks (needlesticks, TB, back).
- **Aging** (at the Year only): from 40, roll **Body**; failure costs −1 to Body or Hands (the
  DM picks, Hands after 50). From 55, roll at −3. A fumble is a diagnosis (hypertension, a
  stent, a tremor, a cancer scare).

## Step 5 — Love and family
- For each close person (partner, children, parents, siblings, lovers), d20: **1–2** something
  hard (an illness, a crisis, a discovery, a divorce petition, a teenager in trouble, a death),
  **19–20** something good (a birth, a reconciliation, an achievement). A neglected
  relationship (he worked every weekend) rolls at −3.
- Children grow. Note their ages at each Year.

## Step 6 — Money (personal)
Salary or dividends, mortgage, school fees (*colegio* and *matrícula*), the car, the lifestyle,
alimony, the cost of a secret. A **Management** roll only if he's in trouble.

## Step 7 — Growth (the Year only)
- Roll for every **✓ trait** and **✓ skill** (`rules.md §6.2, §8.1`). Clear the checks.
- **Training** chosen this year (a course, a fellowship, a year abroad): apply.
- Drives and Standing: adjust for what the year proved.
- Update `characters/player.md`.

---

## The Years Between (fast-forward)

When the player says **"fast forward to [date]"**, or between chapters:
1. For each year skipped, run a **compressed Year**: Steps 1–7 in a few lines, with the rolls.
2. Write **one vignette per year**: 80–200 words in the narrative voice, a single moment that
   stands for the year (a photograph, an argument in a kitchen, a night shift, a funeral, the
   first time a child says something, a night in the empty bunker listening to the Metro pass).
3. Stop the fast-forward early if a roll or the timeline throws up something the player should
   **decide** (a crisis, an offer, an affair, a death). Ask before skipping past a choice.
4. The player can steer the skipped years in advance ("he spends 2008 building the second unit
   and gets divorced") and the DM honours it, rolling only what is uncertain.
