## 2. The setup (default opening: the blackout)
- **How it begins:** Tuesday 25 February 2025, early afternoon, a hot late-summer day. Pietro is
  on an HT shift with Marcela as chief. At ≈15:16 the grid fails across the country.
  - The HT's generators carry it.
  - Next door, **Hora Dorada's Plaza Italia unit's** generator transfer switch fails (deferred
    maintenance under the fund). It has a ventilated patient on battery and an ambulance
    arriving with a crush injury from a Metro evacuation, and its night doctor is twenty-six and
    scared.
  - Marcela sends Pietro over with a portable monitor and a TENS.
  - In the unit's dark basement, looking for the old auxiliary generator Marcela remembers from
    "los simulacros", **an old TENS who was on the list** leads him past the generator room to a
    steel door with a wheel: *"el refugio del doctor Bastías"*.
- **What he knows at the start:** the venture's plan; the rumour that the fund will sell the Plaza
  Italia unit; Marcela's stories of Hora Dorada; nothing about shelters.
- **The question this arc asks:** *what is he willing to buy, and who is paying?*

## 3. The truth (secret)
- B1 "La Capilla" lies beneath the Plaza Italia unit (`era_2_bible.md §3`: Condition 9, sealed
  2020, the 2019 blankets, the Metro shaft door rusted). Its own small generator and batteries
  are dead, but its **hand-cranked ventilation and its lights on a separate battery bank (2019)**
  can be coaxed back, which matters on the blackout night.
- The fund will list the unit in **March 2025**: about 48,000 UF, equipment included, a fire sale.
  Federico Rehbein's company will bid ≈ 55,000 UF through a front (a "clinic investor"), because
  his father wants La Capilla.
- Marcela has half-promised Cáceres an investor (Kostas). Kostas knows Federico Rehbein from a
  Vitacura project, and Federico will try to use that.
- The old TENS: **Nelson Aravena**, 61, who worked at Plaza Italia 2008–2025, on the list,
  remembers the drills and the 2019 nights.

## 6. The discovery tree
- **Path A (he opens the steel door on the blackout night):** La Capilla in torchlight: bunks,
  filtration, the 2019 blankets, a laminated list on the wall dated 2012 with 318 names (Marcela's
  is on it, and Daniela's), a notice in Rodrigo's handwriting, *"Simulacro trimestral — no
  olvidar"*, the Metro shaft's rusted door.
- **Path B (he asks Marcela):** she tells him about the drills and calls it Rodrigo's madness; then
  she wants to know why he's asking.
- **Path C (Josefina's archive):** Carolina's drawings show an "ampliación sala de generadores"
  under Plaza Italia far too big for any generator.
- **Path D (he calls Rodrigo):** see `era_2_bible.md §2`: ambivalent; may tell some, may hang up.
- **Unexpected:** improvise consistently with `era_2_bible.md` and record it here.

## 11. Possible endings
- **Best:** Kairós buys the Plaza Italia unit on fair terms with the father's money capped, and
  Pietro knows what's below and who else wants it.
- **Good:** another site; La Capilla known but not owned.
- **Mixed:** they buy, but Kostas controls the board, or the Rehbein front ends up a partner.
- **Bad:** Rehbein's front wins Plaza Italia; Kairós stalls.
- **Worst:** Marcela's conflict explodes, ACHS fires her, the project collapses.
