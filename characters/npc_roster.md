## Family

### Carolina Ríos Lazcano — his wife
- **Born / age:** June 1971, 33 · **From:** Providencia; a Catholic girls' school; architecture
  at the Universidad de Chile · **Politics:** centre-left, practical
- **Looks and voice:** tall, dark hair always up with a pencil through it, reading glasses she
  hates. Quick, precise, funny when she's not tired; switches to formal Spanish when angry.
- **Work:** architect at a firm that designs hospitals and clinics; knows medical-gas rules,
  seismic codes and DOM permit offices better than most doctors.
- **Relationship:** married Dec 1997. They're good together and tired. She thinks the cellar is
  a harmless obsession inherited from his father, and she wants to design his clinic.
  Told everything on 13 May 2005; will sign the house mortgage on her conditions (no more
  secrets; she designs and sees everything built; if they lose the house she leaves with the
  children). Turned down a partnership at her firm in 2004. **Regard: +4**

## Medicine (colleagues, chiefs, rivals)

### Dr. Arturo Godoy Fuenzalida — his chief at the Posta
- **Born / age:** 1946, 58 · General surgeon, *jefe de turno* at the Posta Central for twenty

- **Looks and voice:** white moustache, cigarettes on the roof, *"el urgenciólogo"* said like an

### Prof. Dr. Tomás Salinas Echeverría — his old surgery professor
- **Born / age:** 1923, 81 · Retired surgeon. Taught surgery at the Universidad de Chile for
  decades (Rodrigo rotated with him in 1991). Founded a private clinic in the 1960s, the
  **Clínica Tajamar** near Plaza Italia, sold years ago.
- **Looks and voice:** tall, stooped, a cane, a tie always; old-fashioned formal Spanish, calls
  everyone *"colega"*.
- **Relationship:** remembered Rodrigo; wrote him a recommendation for the residency because of
  the **1991 blackout in the Salvador's theatre** (the student with the torch who asked about the
  diesel). Hosted the lunch at the Club de la Unión, 19 Apr 2005. Tea at his house (7 May): widower
  (Elena, scrub nurse, d. 2001); on anticoagulants; gave Rodrigo his 1985 miner's cap lamp; told
  him of a false death certificate he signed in 1985. **Regard: +4**
- **Home:** a 1940s ochre house with a lemon tree behind Av. Pedro de Valdivia, Providencia.

### Señora Inés — Salinas's housekeeper and nurse
