# PLAYER CHARACTER — THE FOUNDER (Era I)

## Identity
- **Name:** **Dr. Rodrigo Andrés Bastías Moreno**. "Rorro" to his family, "Bastías" to colleagues,
  "el doctor Bastías" to patients and nurses.
- **Born:** 9 November 1969, Santiago (Hospital del Salvador, where his mother worked) ·
  **Age:** 35 (March 2005)
- **Looks:** 1.78 m, lean, a runner's build. Short dark hair going grey at the temples early;
  brown eyes that look tired even when he isn't; a nose broken in a rugby game at school.
  Shift stubble. Outdoor jacket, jeans, a Casio G-Shock, a pen in every pocket.
- **Voice:** calm, few words, dry humour. Educated middle-class Santiago Spanish; under stress,
  a quiet *"puta la weá"*. With patients, *usted* and patience; with the team, short and clear.
- **From:** raised in the **Villa Olímpica**, Ñuñoa, a middle-class housing estate a few blocks
  from the Estadio Nacional. **Instituto Nacional** (1982–87), the meritocratic public boys'
  school. **Universidad de Chile** medicine (1988–94).

## Why he prepares
His father, **Hernán**, spent thirty years as a radiation-protection technician at the nuclear
research centre in La Reina. Rodrigo grew up with fallout tables on the kitchen table and an old
survey meter in the hall cupboard. In **December 1978**, aged nine, he watched his father tape
the windows, fill the bathtub and sit up all night by the radio while Chile and Argentina came
within hours of war. In **1983**, when Argentina announced it had mastered uranium enrichment,
his father said nothing for a whole dinner. In **1986**, Chernobyl. The bomb has never left him.
He doesn't think it's coming tomorrow. He thinks it's coming, and that when it does, the people
who prepared will be the ones who can help anyone at all.

**Banda Aceh, January–February 2005:** six weeks with an international NGO after the Indian Ocean
tsunami. A gymnasium of the dead, a hospital with no electricity, the difference between the
teams that had planned and the ones that hadn't. He came home three weeks ago with the idea
finished in his head.

## Professional
- **Role (March 2005):** emergency physician at the **Posta Central** (HUAP), public post under
  Ley 15.076 (a 24-hour shift a week plus hours); private shifts in a big clinic's ED; teaches
  residents. Founding his own company.
- **Training:** Universidad de Chile medicine (1988–94); **emergency medicine residency at the
  Universidad de Chile, 1995–98, the programme's second cohort**, one of the first emergency
  specialists in Chile. **SAMU Metropolitano** doctor from its first months (1996–2001):
  regulator at the 131 dispatch and on the mobile units. Disaster-medicine diploma course
  abroad (2002). ATLS and PHTLS instructor.
- **Experience:** ten years of the Posta's nights; SAMU's first ambulances; Aceh.
- **Reputation:** *"el Bastías de urgencia"*, a pioneer of a tiny specialty (there are perhaps
  50 emergency specialists in Chile); calm in a crisis; a bit odd about earthquakes and bombs;
  good with money, which colleagues find suspicious.

## Life
- **Home:** a 1950s house off Plaza Ñuñoa, bought 2001 (UF mortgage). The **cellar** is his:
  three months of food and water for four, a shortwave radio, his father's old survey meter,
  medical kits. Carolina calls it *"tu búnker"* and doesn't go down there.
- **Car:** green 2003 Subaru Forester; a go-bag and a trauma kit in the back.
- **Love:** married to **Carolina Ríos Lazcano** (architect, hospital design) since December 1997.
- **Children:** **Antonia** (6, born February 1999) and **Vicente** (2, born September 2002).
- **Parents:** **Hernán Bastías Rojas** (63, retired radiation-protection technician, ham-radio
  operator) and **Gladys Moreno Tapia** (60, retired nurse, Hospital del Salvador), still in the
  Villa Olímpica flat.
- **Sister:** **Javiera Bastías Moreno** (30), a reporter at a Santiago radio newsroom.
- **Friend:** Dr. Sebastián "Seba" Contreras (co-resident, SAMU, his first partner).
- **Rival:** Dr. Arturo Godoy, the surgeon who runs his shift at the Posta and despises "the
  urgenciólogos".
- **Debt:** Patricio "Pato" Leiva bet on him early; Rodrigo owes him loyalty, and a seat.
- **Secret:** since 2003 he has been quietly taking near-expiry stock from MedSur's warehouse
  (Pato's company), controlled drugs included (morphine, ketamine, midazolam), and storing it in
  his cellar and in a rented storage unit in Quilicura. Pato doesn't know about the controlled
  drugs. It's a crime.
