# ARC 2 — Kairós — What Pietro knows

## The situation as he understands it
With Marcela Riquelme, his former residency chief and now head of the Hospital del Trabajador's
ED, he is founding **Kairós**, a stabilization centre on the Hora Dorada model, without a fund
behind it. The company exists; the plan is done; the site and the money are not. Hora Dorada,
the network that invented the model, is being cut by the fund that owns it.
