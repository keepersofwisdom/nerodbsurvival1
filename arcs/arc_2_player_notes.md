# ARC 2 — Kairós — What Pietro knows

## The situation as he understands it
With Marcela Riquelme, his former residency chief and now head of the Hospital del Trabajador's
ED, he is founding **Kairós**, a stabilization centre on the Hora Dorada model, without a fund
behind it. The company exists; the plan is done; the site and the money are not. Hora Dorada,
the network that invented the model, is being cut by the fund that owns it.

A barrel-vaulted hall. Aravena: "Le decían la Capilla, por el techo. El doctor Bastías decía que le venía de antes. Yo nunca le pregunté de antes de qué." Inside:
  - folded steel bunks three high; the 2019 blankets; stretchers;

  - water dated 2021, rations, a padlocked medical cabinet;
  - a steel ventilation unit with German labels and a hand crank;
  - a chained, rusted door: *METRO — L5 — EMERGENCIA*;
  - **a list on the wall**: "LISTA — PERSONAL Y GRUPO FAMILIAR — RED HORA DORADA — actualizada marzo 2012", hundreds of names, including **Marcela Riquelme Soto (+1, her son)**, **Daniela Fuentes (+2)**, **Nelson Aravena (+3)**;

  - the side room: a battery bank and inverter wired by an orange cable to the critical-care room (2019), kept on float charge. At 91% it powered the critical-care room through the blackout.
