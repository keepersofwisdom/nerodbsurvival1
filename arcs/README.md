# ARCS — PLAYER NOTES

One file per arc: `arc_N_player_notes.md`. This is **what the player character has discovered
and believes**: clues, rumours, people met, promises, open questions. It's written from his
side of the story and safe for the player to read.

In **Era II**, the heir starts with their own notes: what the heir knows is not what the
founder knew, even if the player remembers. The DM's secret plan for each arc is in
`dm/arc_N_bible.md` (do not read).
