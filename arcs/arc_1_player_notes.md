# ARC 1 — La Primera Piedra — What the Founder knows

## The situation as he understands it
Back from Banda Aceh in February 2005 with the idea finished: a flagship clinic in the civic
district, then small stabilization units across Santiago, tied together by ambulances. Get the
patient, stabilize, transfer. He has three partners and about 14,500 UF between them. A
flagship needs something like 120,000–170,000 UF. Banks lend half against property. The rest
he'll have to find.

## Things learned (with session numbers)
- (S1) Three buildings for sale near La Moneda, viewed 31 Mar 2005 with Fierro and Gonzalo:
  - **Torre Teatinos** (1974): 46,000 UF; asbestos ceilings, 2.40 m ceilings, small lift. All-in ≈144,000 UF.
  - **Old bank, calle Moneda** (1920s): 44,000 UF; banking hall, skylight, great vault; heritage-protected, a year of permits, narrow street 40 m from the palace. All-in ≈121,000 UF.
  - **Edificio Caja Austral** (1938), Agustinas between Teatinos and Amunátegui: 62,000 UF; carriage entrance to an interior courtyard (ambulance bay), stretcher-size freight lift, 3.60 m ceilings, 2 basements. All-in ≈152,000 UF. Gonzalo: "too cheap" (≈14.8 UF/m²). Seller: **Rentas Santa Elena Ltda.**, "a family estate".
- (S1) In the Caja Austral's basement −2: a filled-in brick **arch** painted over more recently than the rest of the wall. The watchman, **Pincheira** (there "since the military left", 1990; a small castle tattooed on his forearm), went quiet and watched while Rodrigo stood in front of it.
- (S1) Gonzalo's numbers: partners 14,500 UF + MedSur equipment credit ≈15,000 + bank 55–60% of property and works → a gap of **40,000–55,000 UF** in every option, before ~6 months of working capital.

- (S1) Offer of **55,000 UF** for the Caja Austral (subject to title study and bank financing; *promesa* within 30 days) was **accepted in under three days**, on a weekend. The written acceptance came from the law firm **Covarrubias, Prieto & Asociados** (El Golf), signed "por Rentas Santa Elena Ltda." and "**por encargo de Inversiones Huelén Ltda.**" (Huelén = the old name of Cerro Santa Lucía). Deposit 10% = 5,500 UF.
- (S1) The "other buyers from Meiggs" Fierro mentioned never appeared.
- (S1) Partners' dinner (2 Apr): Seba wants to be clinical director ("no quiero ser tu empleado"); Gonzalo wants a lawyer-drafted pact with exit and drag-along clauses, +3% for running finance full-time, and no *promesa* without a title study; Pato wants 5 years' exclusive supply, offers equipment at cost on credit (and a "reconditioned" Bird ventilator). Nothing settled.
- (S1) Carolina's verdict on the Caja Austral: "este edificio fue hecho para esto": ED on the ground floor by the courtyard, ORs on the 2nd, ICU on the 3rd; the damp in basement −2 needs waterproofing and a groundwater study before digging deeper.

- (S1) **Gonzalo's findings (8 Apr):** the Caja Austral was built in 1938 by a regional pension fund (*caja de previsión*); after the 1980 pension reform it passed to the **INP (the state)**, which sold it in **January 1990**, two months before Aylwin took office, to **Rentas Santa Elena Ltda.** for **9,000 UF**. Santa Elena (1989) belongs to **María Elena Correa** and her children; she's a cousin of **don Anselmo Larrañaga Correa**, whose family company is **Inversiones Huelén Ltda.** (1979; domiciled at the law firm in El Golf). Larrañaga: textiles, then banking in the 1980s; on boards during the 1983 bank interventions and came out well; close to the regime, low profile; over 80. **Title is clean.** The "Chinese buyers from Meiggs" were invented: nobody else asked about the building in two years.
- (S1) Bank (7 Apr): Verónica Saldías needs the full business plan, 3-year projections, the *promesa* and the works budget; guarantees would be the building's mortgage plus the partners' personal guarantees, and probably a second mortgage on the Ñuñoa house. Committee in 3–4 weeks.
- (S1) Hora Dorada S.A. constituted (13 Apr); shareholders' pact signed; *promesa* signed at the notary on Huérfanos (14 Apr), deposit 5,500 UF. The seller's lawyer: "Es un edificio con mucha historia."
- (S1) **15 Apr:** a handwritten invitation from **Prof. Tomás Salinas** to lunch at the **Club de la Unión**, Tue 19 Apr, 13:30, with "un amigo, don Anselmo Larrañaga Correa, que tiene mucho interés en conocerlo".

- (S1) **Lunch at the Club de la Unión (19 Apr 2005)** with Prof. Salinas and don Anselmo Larrañaga (84):
  - Salinas remembered the **1991 blackout in the Salvador's theatre**: Rodrigo, a 4th-year student, held a torch over the field for eleven minutes, then asked how much diesel the hospital had. That's why Salinas wrote his residency letter.
  - Larrañaga knew about Gonzalo's week in the Conservador; admitted his cousin sold the building, bought cheap in Jan 1990.
  - Larrañaga's fear: the night of 21 Dec 1978 (the state had "ni un sótano"); Argentina's enrichment announcement (Pilcaniyeu, Nov 1983); "la Guerra Fría no terminó, se fue a dormir". He knew of Hernán ("se hablaba bien de él" in La Reina) and of the Ñuñoa cellar, "una botica bastante completa", and saw Rodrigo's face move.
  - **The offer:** Inversiones Huelén, up to **45,000 UF**, either a **15-year loan at UF+2%** or **non-voting preferred shares**. **Conditions:** (1) the deep works under the flagship and every future unit designed by *their* engineer, "un general de ingenieros, retirado", and paid by them, presented as seismic works, parking, generators, water; (2) **a shelter beneath for them and their families**, with a year of stores; (3) **absolute discretion**, in writing and by word; (4) **a place on their list** for Rodrigo, Carolina and the children.
  - Rodrigo committed to nothing: any money goes to the board and the lawyer; asked for it in writing. Larrañaga: "Tómese el tiempo que necesite... mientras no sea mucho. Los dos sabemos cuánto dura una promesa de compraventa."
  - Rodrigo's read: Salinas is warm and sincere, but shame or fear shows at "certificados" ("algunos no se olvidan nunca") and at "su papá"; Larrañaga weighs him like an asset. They're not fully aligned: Larrañaga cut Salinas off ("Hay cosas que hicimos que…" / "Tomás." / "…tarde y mal"). "Nosotros / nuestras familias": there are more of them.

- (S1) **Board meeting (20 Apr 2005)**, partners + Molina. Seba: "Viejos con búnker... ¿Y la lista? ¿Y la Ximena, los TENS, los choferes?" He won't sign a shelter that leaves the team outside. Pato: "un regalo del cielo", yes and fast; MedSur can bring generators, tanks and air filters from Germany. Gonzalo: as money it's excellent (half the bank's cost); the problem is condition 2. A registered right over the subsoil kills the bank loan; a private paper is worth only their word. Preferred shares need 75%, and **Rodrigo + Pato = 79%**. He prefers the loan ("un acreedor se va cuando le pagas; un socio no se va nunca"). Molina: no legal figure for "a shelter for third parties"; the clean option is a **real long-term lease of underground storage to Huelén, subordinate to the bank's mortgage**, plus a commercial confidentiality clause; "lo de la lista no se escribe en ningún contrato que yo vaya a firmar". Minutes: request a written proposal; the chairman has a mandate to negotiate and report.
