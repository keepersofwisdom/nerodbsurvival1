# RESEARCH COMPENDIUM — INDEX

This folder is the real-world backbone of the campaign. It is **public**: the player may read
any of it (it's the real world, not plot). The DM reads the relevant file before running any
scene in a new place, institution or year.

Nothing here is a plot secret. Plot secrets live in `dm/` and `characters/npc_secrets.md`.

| File | What it covers | Read it when… |
|---|---|---|
| `01_santiago_history.md` | Santiago from 1541 to 2005: the class geography of the comunas, the 20th century, the coup hour by hour, the dictatorship's detention sites and clinics (Clínica Santa Lucía, Clínica London, Borgoño…), doctors under the dictatorship, the Frei Montalva case, the transition, "old money" as social types | Any scene touching the past, the dictatorship, old men, memory sites, family histories |
| `02_chile_timeline_2005_2026.md` | Year by year, Jan 2005 → Oct 2026: politics, disasters, health, Santiago, economy (UF, dollar, minimum wage), tech | **Every time time passes.** The source of `world/timeline.md` |
| `03_underground_santiago.md` | What really lies beneath: La Moneda's bunker hall and the SICAR vault under the Plaza de la Constitución, Améstica's *Santiago Subterráneo*, colonial legends, DINA/CNI basements, Metro (ghost stations, depths), the San Borja service tunnels, canals and sewers, hospital tunnels, the gravel and groundwater, permits, spoil, covers for deep concrete | Any scene underground; any bunker work; any investigation of the tunnels |
| `04_chile_health_system.md` | Fonasa, Isapres, mutuales; the **Ley de Urgencia** in detail; GES; permits, regulation and accreditation for a clinic and an ambulance service; money (costs, salaries, tariffs); the professional landscape | Business scenes, billing, inspections, lawsuits, negotiations with payers |
| `05_emergency_medicine_ems_disaster.md` | History of emergency medicine in Chile; SAMU and private rescue services; ambulance classes; the big EDs and their culture; disaster medicine and case studies (27F, San Miguel, the estallido, COVID, Viña 2024, the blackout); trauma epidemiology | Every clinical scene; every disaster; building the founder and the heir |
| `06_gazetteer_central_santiago.md` | The neighbourhoods (2005 / 2019 / 2025), the hospital and clinic directory, travel times, commercial real-estate prices | Choosing sites, ambulance runs, transfers, any new neighbourhood |
| `07_infrastructure_and_catastrophe.md` | Santiago's lifelines (water, power, fuel, food, comms, transport) and how they fail; the scenarios (nuclear war, solar storm, pandemic, San Ramón fault, volcanoes, drought) hour by hour; bunker engineering, stores, Chilean law on guns, fuel and digging; prepper culture | The founder's preparations; any outage or disaster; Era III |
| `08_daily_life_and_flavour.md` | Chilean Spanish (≈200 entries by register and generation), hospital slang and culture, prices by year, technology and culture by year, food, weather, holidays, class markers, crime, names, inspiration list | **Every scene.** The texture file |

## Accuracy notes

- The research was compiled in **October 2026** with partial web access (some sites blocked, a
  search budget that ran out). Each file states its method and **tags claims by reliability**
  (documented / reported / background knowledge / estimate / legend). Respect the tags.
- **Legend is labelled as legend.** The tunnels under Santiago are partly real and partly
  rumour; the files say which is which. The game's fiction lives in the gaps.
- **Real people and places:** crimes and secrets in the fiction belong to fictional people and
  places invented *beside* the real ones (see `config.md`).
- When something matters and isn't in these files, the DM consults the **`researcher`** or the
  **`clinician`** agent, which may search the web, and then **adds the finding here**.
