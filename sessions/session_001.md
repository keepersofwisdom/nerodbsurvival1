- **Real dates:** 26 Oct – 2 Nov 2026
- **In-game span:** Thu 31 Mar 2005, Posta Central → Fri 27 May 2005, Ñuñoa; then Era I written
  forward to Aug 2023
- **Era / arc:** I — arc 1, La Primera Piedra (closed)
- **Character played:** the Founder, Dr. Rodrigo Bastías

## Summary
Rodrigo came off a 24-hour shift at the Posta Central and viewed three buildings for sale
beside La Moneda. In the basement of the 1938 Edificio Caja Austral he noticed a brick arch
painted more recently than the wall around it, and an old watchman, Pincheira, watching him
stand in front of it. He made a low offer of 55,000 UF. It was accepted in under three days by a
"family estate", and the acceptance slipped the name **Inversiones Huelén**. Gonzalo traced
Huelén to **don Anselmo Larrañaga**, an old banker of the 1980s, and found that the state had sold
the building to Larrañaga's cousin in January 1990 for 9,000 UF.

The four partners founded **Hora Dorada S.A.** (Rodrigo 62%). They signed the purchase agreement
and paid a 5,500 UF deposit. Rodrigo's old surgery professor, **Salinas**, invited him to lunch at
the Club de la Unión with Larrañaga. There, over the day Benedict XVI was elected, the old men
told him about the Beagle night of 1978 and the bomb, let him know they knew about his cellar,
and offered 45,000 UF on four conditions. Their engineer would dig beneath his clinics, there
would be a shelter for them and theirs, absolute discretion, and a place on their list for his
family.

Rodrigo put it all before his partners. The bank approved 63,250 UF on hard terms: his house,
and no third-party rights on the building. Over tea at his house, Salinas confessed a false
death certificate signed in 1985, said "no todo lo que va a encontrar ahí abajo va a ser nuevo",
and gave Rodrigo the miner's lamp he'd operated by in the 1985 earthquake. Molina used the bank's
clause to strip Huelén's annex almost bare ("Bien negociado, doctor"). Rodrigo told Carolina
everything. She agreed to sign the mortgage on three conditions, and told him of the
partnership she'd given up in 2004.

Larrañaga refused to let Carolina review the deep works, but she signed his confidentiality to
see them. At General Benavente's house the real −3 lay on the table: a fallout shelter for forty
under the courtyard, and a hatched corner marked "estructura existente, no intervenir", which
Carolina knew was no water tank. It was exactly behind the arch.

At the player's direction, Era I was then written forward to 2023 (`world/chronicle_2005_2023.md`).
